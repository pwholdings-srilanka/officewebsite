Does Zoho Books support Sri Lanka VAT (18%) and SSCL (2.5%) tax compliance? +

Yes, 100%. Zoho Books configured by PW Holdings provides full Inland Revenue Department (IRD) compliance. Zoho One is the unified "Operating System for Business" containing 45+ enterprise-grade cloud applications under a single license.
