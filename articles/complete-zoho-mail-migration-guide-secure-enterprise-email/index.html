💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Will our company lose any emails during the switch to Zoho Mail?

No. Because we migrate data via server-to-server IMAP prior to updating MX records and keep secondary queues alive, not a single email or calendar event is lost during migration.

Q2: Can we use our existing company domain (e.g., yourname@yourcompany.com)?

Yes. Zoho Mail is designed specifically for custom business domains. You can host multiple domains and manage unlimited domain aliases under a single central organization account.

Q3: How does Zoho Mail protect against phishing and spam?

Zoho Mail utilizes multi-layered security protocols, including SPF validation, DKIM signature verification, DMARC compliance monitoring, advanced antispam filtering, and opportunistic TLS encryption.
