Zoho Books Sri Lanka VAT & SSCL Setup: Complete 2026 IRD Tax Guide

By PW Holdings Senior ERP Consultants • Published September 2026 • 6 Min Read

⚡ Quick Takeaway (AI Overview Summary)

Sri Lankan businesses registered for Value Added Tax (VAT 18%) and Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL 2.5%) can fully automate tax calculations in Zoho Books. By creating custom compound tax groups and setting up automated tax invoices with IRD TIN/VAT numbers, companies eliminate manual tax adjustments and generate audit-ready quarterly returns seamlessly.

1. Why Sri Lankan Businesses Require Localized Zoho Books Setup

With ongoing updates by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Sri Lanka regarding VAT thresholds and SSCL reporting, managing invoices on spreadsheets or standalone desktop software leads to costly compliance penalties.

Zoho Books provides an enterprise-ready cloud platform that handles:

  • Automated VAT (18%) & SSCL (2.5%) Calculation on standard rated goods and services.
  • Customer TIN and VAT Registration Number Display on printable PDF invoices.
  • Withholding Tax (WHT / Advance Income Tax) deduction at source for vendor payments.
  • Multi-Currency Bank Reconciliation with major Sri Lankan commercial banks.

2. Step-by-Step VAT & SSCL Configuration

Step 1: Set Currency and Base Locale

Navigate to Settings → Organization Profile and set Base Currency to LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee) with Colombo time zone (+05:30).

Step 2: Create Custom Tax Rates

Go to Settings → Taxes → Tax Rates and add:

  • SSCL (2.5%) - Applied on the turnover base value.
  • VAT (18.0%) - Applied on the post-SSCL taxable value (or standard base).

Step 3: Create Compound Tax Group (VAT + SSCL)

Create a Tax Group named "VAT 18% + SSCL 2.5%". Zoho Books automatically applies the correct compound logic so your customer invoices reflect the exact breakdown required by IRD guidelines.
