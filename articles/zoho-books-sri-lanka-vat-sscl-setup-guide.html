Zoho Books Sri Lanka VAT & SSCL Setup: Complete 2026 IRD Tax Guide

Comprehensive IRD tax compliance setup, custom tax rates, withholding taxes, automated tax returns, and digital invoice generation for Sri Lankan businesses.

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) Compliance Requirements

Operating a business in Sri Lanka requires strict adherence to Inland Revenue Department (IRD) regulations. With Value Added Tax (VAT) standing at 18% and the Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL) at 2.5%, improper tax accounting can result in severe penalties and cash flow disruption. Zoho Books provides dedicated tax localization configured by PW Holdings to automate calculations at point of sale.

Configuring Tax Rates & Compound Calculations

In Sri Lanka, SSCL is calculated on the liable turnover, followed by VAT on the combined value in specific industries. Inside Zoho Books, we set up hierarchical tax groups, automated exemption categories for export revenue, and digital tax invoices containing the mandatory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration numbers.

Automating RAMIS Returns & Financial Statements

At the close of every tax period, Zoho Books generates detailed VAT and SSCL summary reports compatible with the Revenue Administration Management Information System (RAMIS). This eliminates manual spreadsheets, shortens audit preparation time, and gives CFOs full visibility into input and output tax positions.

A: Yes. When set up by PW Holdings, Zoho Books automatically applies 18% VAT and 2.5% SSCL on invoices, handles exempt goods, and prints legally compliant IRD tax invoices.

Q: Can Zoho Books generate RAMIS tax return summaries?
