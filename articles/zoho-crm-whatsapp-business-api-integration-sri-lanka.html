💡 WhatsApp CRM Automation: The Modern Sales Engine for Sri Lankan Companies

1. Why WhatsApp Is the #1 B2B & B2C Communication Channel in Sri Lanka

Over 80% of customer inquiries in Sri Lanka occur over WhatsApp. However, when sales representatives communicate via personal phones, customer data is lost, response times lag, and management lacks visibility into the deal pipeline. Integrating official WhatsApp Business API with Zoho CRM resolves this completely.

2. Instant Lead Capture & Automated Quotations

When a prospect messages your official WhatsApp number, Zoho CRM automatically creates a new lead record, assigns it to the appropriate sales executive based on territory, and logs conversation history. Sales agents can generate PDF quotations from Zoho Books and send them directly inside WhatsApp in seconds.

3. Zia AI Chatbots & Customer Service with Zoho SalesIQ

By pairing WhatsApp with Zoho SalesIQ chatbots powered by Zia AI, businesses can answer customer FAQs 24/7, route support tickets to Zoho Desk, and automatically update payment status once invoices are cleared.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can multiple team members manage one WhatsApp Business number in Zoho CRM?

Yes. With Zoho CRM WhatsApp Business API integration, your entire sales and support team can access a unified shared inbox using a single verified company number.

Q2: Is conversation history saved on the customer record?

Yes, every incoming and outgoing message, image, and quotation is automatically logged on the customer's CRM profile for full auditability.
